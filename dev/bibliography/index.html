Bibliography

The articles the methods of TMS come from, each with what it gives TMS. To cite TMS itself, see References.

Matrix product states and operators

  • M. Fishman, S. R. White and E. M. Stoudenmire, The ITensor Software Library for Tensor Network Calculations, SciPost Phys. Codebases 4 (2022), doi:10.21468/SciPostPhysCodeb.4, arXiv:2007.14822: the tensors, the MPS and MPO and the solvers TMS is built on.
  • S. R. White, Density matrix formulation for quantum renormalization groups, Phys. Rev. Lett. 69, 2863 (1992), doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.69.2863: DMRG, which Dmrg runs through ITensorMPS.
  • C. Hubig, I. P. McCulloch and U. Schollwöck, Generic construction of efficient matrix product operators, Phys. Rev. B 95, 035129 (2017), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.95.035129, arXiv:1611.02498: the terms of an operator compacted into as few channels as their rank, the sums and products of PreMPO, the factors rescaled by powers of two.
  • D. E. Parker, X. Cao and M. P. Zaletel, Local matrix product operators: Canonical form, compression, and control theory, Phys. Rev. B 102, 035147 (2020), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.102.035147, arXiv:1909.06341: the truncation of an MPO by mpo_limits, on an orthonormal basis of each site, the identity apart.

Mixed states

  • F. Verstraete, J. J. García-Ripoll and J. I. Cirac, Matrix Product Density Operators: Simulation of Finite-Temperature and Dissipative Systems, Phys. Rev. Lett. 93, 207204 (2004), doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.93.207204, arXiv:cond-mat/0406426, and M. Zwolak and G. Vidal, Mixed-State Dynamics in One-Dimensional Quantum Lattice Systems: A Time-Dependent Superoperator Renormalization Algorithm, Phys. Rev. Lett. 93, 207205 (2004), doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.93.207205, arXiv:cond-mat/0406440: the mixed representation, an MPS of the vectorized density matrix acted on by superoperators, and thermalize, its evolution in imaginary time.
  • A. H. Werner, D. Jaschke, P. Silvi, M. Kliesch, T. Calarco, J. Eisert and S. Montangero, Positive Tensor Network Approach for Simulating Open Quantum Many-Body Systems, Phys. Rev. Lett. 116, 237201 (2016), doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.116.237201, arXiv:1412.5746, and C. D. White, M. Zaletel, R. S. K. Mong and G. Refael, Quantum dynamics of thermalizing systems, Phys. Rev. B 97, 035127 (2018), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.97.035127, arXiv:1707.01506: what the cutoff of a density matrix bounds, see Truncation.

Time evolution

  • J. Haegeman, J. I. Cirac, T. J. Osborne, I. Pižorn, H. Verschelde and F. Verstraete, Time-Dependent Variational Principle for Quantum Lattices, Phys. Rev. Lett. 107, 070601 (2011), doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.107.070601, arXiv:1103.0936, and J. Haegeman, C. Lubich, I. Oseledets, B. Vandereycken and F. Verstraete, Unifying time evolution and optimization with matrix product states, Phys. Rev. B 94, 165116 (2016), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.94.165116, arXiv:1408.5056: the time dependent variational principle, which Tdvp runs through ITensorMPS.
  • M. Yang and S. R. White, Time-dependent variational principle with ancillary Krylov subspace, Phys. Rev. B 102, 094315 (2020), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.102.094315, arXiv:2005.06104: the global Krylov expansion Tdvp makes before a step from a small bond dimension.
  • M. P. Zaletel, R. S. K. Mong, C. Karrasch, J. E. Moore and F. Pollmann, Time-evolving a matrix product state with long-ranged interactions, Phys. Rev. B 91, 165112 (2015), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.91.165112, arXiv:1407.1832: the approximations WI and WII of ApproxW, and its order 2.
  • K. Bidzhiev and G. Misguich, Out-of-equilibrium dynamics in a quantum impurity model: Numerics for particle transport and entanglement entropy, Phys. Rev. B 96, 195117 (2017), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.96.195117, arXiv:1707.06111, appendix A: the complex steps of ApproxW of orders 3 and 4.
  • S. Blanes and P. C. Moan, Fourth- and sixth-order commutator-free Magnus integrators for linear and non-linear dynamical systems, Appl. Numer. Math. 56, 1519 (2006), doi:10.1016/j.apnum.2005.11.004: the commutator-free Magnus integrator of order 4 of the evolvers depending on time.
  • E. M. Stoudenmire and S. R. White, Minimally entangled typical thermal state algorithms, New J. Phys. 12, 055026 (2010), doi:10.1088/1367-2630/12/5/055026, arXiv:1002.1305, and C. Camaño, E. N. Epperly and J. A. Tropp, Successive randomized compression: A randomized algorithm for the compressed MPO-MPS product, Quantum 10, 2022 (2026), doi:10.22331/q-2026-03-10-2022, arXiv:2504.06475: the zip-up product of apply_algo = "zipup", and how the products of an MPO by a state compare, "densitymatrix" included.

Searches

  • J. Cui, J. I. Cirac and M. C. Bañuls, Variational Matrix Product Operators for the Steady State of Dissipative Quantum Systems, Phys. Rev. Lett. 114, 220601 (2015), doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.114.220601, arXiv:1501.06786: steady_state, by dmrg on $L^\dagger L$.

States and measurements

  • M. T. Fishman and S. R. White, Compression of correlation matrices and an efficient method for forming matrix product states of fermionic Gaussian states, Phys. Rev. B 92, 075132 (2015), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.92.075132, arXiv:1504.07701: the circuit of rotations that builds slater_state and fermi_sea.
  • A. J. Ferris and G. Vidal, Perfect sampling with unitary tensor networks, Phys. Rev. B 85, 165146 (2012), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.85.165146, arXiv:1201.3974: the perfect sampling of sample on a pure state, through ITensorMPS.
  • H. Shapourian, K. Shiozaki and S. Ryu, Partial time-reversal transformation and entanglement negativity in fermionic systems, Phys. Rev. B 95, 165101 (2017), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.95.165101, arXiv:1611.07536: the fermionic partial transpose, which log_negativity does not compute, refusing two parts holding fermions.
