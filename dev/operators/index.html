Usage

The examples of this page use qubits on a six site system, that is

using TensorMixedStates, .Qubits, .Circuits

n = 6
j = 1.0
h = 0.5

There are two kinds of operators: generic (like X) and indexed (like X(3)). Indexed operators are applied to specific site numbers.

Operators define Hamiltonians, for example

hamiltonian = - j * sum(X(i)X(i+1) + Y(i)Y(i+1) for i in 1:n-1) - h * sum(Z(i) for i in 1:n)
-X(1)*X(2)-Y(1)*Y(2)-X(2)*X(3)-Y(2)*Y(3)-X(3)*X(4)-Y(3)*Y(4)-X(4)*X(5)-Y(4)*Y(5)-X(5)*X(6)-Y(5)*Y(6)-0.5Z(1)-0.5Z(2)-0.5Z(3)-0.5Z(4)-0.5Z(5)-0.5Z(6)

or Lindbladian dissipators like

dissipators = sum(Dissipator(Sp)(i) for i in 1:n)
Dissipator(Sp)(1)+Dissipator(Sp)(2)+Dissipator(Sp)(3)+Dissipator(Sp)(4)+Dissipator(Sp)(5)+Dissipator(Sp)(6)

to build a Lindbladian

lindbladian = -im * hamiltonian + dissipators
Evolver(im*X(1)*X(2)+im*Y(1)*Y(2)+im*X(2)*X(3)+im*Y(2)*Y(3)+im*X(3)*X(4)+im*Y(3)*Y(4)+im*X(4)*X(5)+im*Y(4)*Y(5)+im*X(5)*X(6)+im*Y(5)*Y(6)+0.5im*Z(1)+0.5im*Z(2)+0.5im*Z(3)+0.5im*Z(4)+0.5im*Z(5)+0.5im*Z(6))+Dissipator(Sp)(1)+Dissipator(Sp)(2)+Dissipator(Sp)(3)+Dissipator(Sp)(4)+Dissipator(Sp)(5)+Dissipator(Sp)(6)

Note the factor -im for the Hamiltonian.

They define quantum gates, the module Circuits giving H, the rotations and the controlled gates, like

gates = H(1)Swap(1, 2)H(1)
H(1)*Swap(1,2)*H(1)

Noisy gates can be defined using the Gate constructor, for example

noisygate = 0.7Gate(Id) + 0.1Gate(X) + 0.1Gate(Y) + 0.1Gate(Z)
0.7Gate(Id)+0.1Gate(X)+0.1Gate(Y)+0.1Gate(Z)

The reset of sites of any type to a state is the channel reset_channel, and its Lindblad generator, for an evolver, reset_dissipator; to the fully mixed state, it is the depolarization, depolarizing_channel and depolarizing_dissipator. The dephasing channel and its generator are dephasing_channel and dephasing_dissipator, built on Dephase, and the decay of the excitations an operator destroys, amplitude_damping_channel and amplitude_damping_dissipator.

Two conventions differ from one text to another. depolarizing_channel(p) replaces the state of a site by the fully mixed one with probability p, $(1 - p)\rho + p\,I/d$: on a qubit, the channel $(1 - p)\rho + \frac{p}{3}(X \rho X + Y \rho Y + Z \rho Z)$, which applies each Pauli operator with probability $p/3$, is depolarizing_channel(4p/3). On a qubit, "Up" is $|0\rangle$ and "Dn" is $|1\rangle$, the excitation that N counts and that Sp destroys: amplitude_damping_channel and amplitude_damping_dissipator take "Dn" to "Up".

And they define observables

obs = X(1)X(2)Z(3)
X(1)*X(2)*Z(3)

Reference

Complex operators can be built from a rich set of functions, for example

Rxy(t) = exp(-im * t * (X⊗X + Y⊗Y) / 4)
Rxy (generic function with 1 method)
Rxy(0.2)(2, 5)
exp(-0.05im*X⊗X-0.05im*Y⊗Y)(2,5)

Operators can be added and multiplied using usual operators (+, -, *, /, ^).

TensorMixedStates.Proj — Type
Proj(state)
Proj(A => λ)

the projector $|s\rangle\langle s|$ on a local pure state of one site, given as State takes it, a vector standing for its unit vector, Proj([1, 1]) projecting on $(|0\rangle + |1\rangle)/\sqrt 2$, or the projector on the eigenspace of eigenvalue λ of the Hermitian operator A, of one site or several. TensorMixedStates.Dissipator — Type
Dissipator(L)
Dissipator(Φ)

the Lindblad dissipator of the jump operator L, generic or placed on sites, $\rho \mapsto L\rho L^\dagger - \frac{1}{2}\{L^\dagger L, \rho\}$, or that of the channel Φ, the sum of those of its Kraus operators, $\Phi - \mathrm{id}$ for Φ preserving the trace. Dissipator(c * L) is abs2(c) * Dissipator(L), and Dissipator(Sm(1)) is Dissipator(Sm)(1).

Examples

Dissipator(Sm)(1)
Dissipator(Sm(1) + Sm(2))           # a collective decay

source
TensorMixedStates.Gate — Type
Gate(A)

the channel $\rho \mapsto A\rho A^\dagger$ of the operator A, generic or placed on sites: Gate(X(1)) is Gate(X)(1), Gate(c * A) is abs2(c) * Gate(A), and the gate of a product is the product of the gates. Channels combine with weights of zero or above, which is how a noisy gate is written. An operator multiplied by a channel, or in a tensor product with one, becomes its gate.

Examples

0.9 * Gate(Id) + 0.1 * Gate(X)      # X with probability 0.1
Gate(X(1) + X(2))
source
TensorMixedStates.Reset — Type
Reset(state)

the channel resetting a site to the local state state, given as State takes it, $\rho \mapsto \sigma \otimes \mathrm{tr}_i \rho$, $\sigma$ the density matrix of state and $\mathrm{tr}_i$ the trace over the site. On a fermionic site this holds on states of definite parity: on the others, an odd operator O of the other sites takes the value of $\langle F\rangle_\sigma F_i O$, $F_i$ the parity of the site. A vector stands for its unit vector and a matrix for itself divided by its trace, Reset([1, 1]) resetting to $(|0\rangle + |1\rangle)/\sqrt 2$. An operator of one site placed on it places the reset there: Reset(N(3) => 1) is Reset(N => 1)(3).

Examples

Reset("Up")(3)
Reset(Level => 0)(3)
Reset([0.3 0. ; 0. 0.7])(3)
source
TensorMixedStates.Dephase — Type
Dephase(A)

the channel dephasing in the eigenbasis of the Hermitian operator A, of one site or several, $\rho \mapsto \sum_\lambda P_\lambda \rho P_\lambda$, $P_\lambda$ the projector on the eigenspace of A of eigenvalue $\lambda$: the measurement of A whose result is not read. It erases the coherences between eigenspaces and keeps those within one. An operator placed on sites places the dephasing there: Dephase(N(1)) is Dephase(N)(1).

Examples

Dephase(Level)(3)       # in the basis of the site
Dephase(Ntot)(2)        # on an Electron, keeps the coherence of Up and Dn
Dephase(Z ⊗ Z)(1, 2)    # the parity of two qubits, not read
source

reset_channel(p, state)

the channel resetting a site, n sites, or as many sites as states, with probability p to state, or to a state per site, the sites being reset together: $(1 - p)\,\rho + p\,\sigma \otimes \mathrm{tr}_S\rho$.

Examples

reset_channel(0.1, "Up")(3)

source
TensorMixedStates.reset_dissipator — Function
reset_dissipator(γ, state)
reset_dissipator(γ, n, state)
reset_dissipator(γ, [state1, state2, ...])

the generator resetting a site, n sites, or as many sites as states, at rate γ to state, or to a state per site, the sites being reset together: γ * Dissipator(Reset(state)), $\gamma\,(\sigma \otimes \mathrm{tr}_S\rho - \rho)$. A state is given as for Reset, and a vector of numbers is the amplitudes of a single state. Evolving under it for a time t is reset_channel(1 - exp(-γt), ...).

Examples

-im * H + sum(reset_dissipator(0.1, "Up")(i) for i in 1:10)
source
TensorMixedStates.depolarizing_channel — Function
depolarizing_channel(p, n = 1)

the channel depolarizing n sites together with probability p, reset_channel(p, n, "FullyMixed"), $(1 - p)\,\rho + p\,\mathrm{tr}_S\rho \otimes I/d^n$: on a qubit, (1 - 3p/4) * Gate(Id) + p/4 * (Gate(X) + Gate(Y) + Gate(Z)). Two sites depolarized together are not two sites depolarized each on its own, depolarizing_channel(p) ⊗ depolarizing_channel(p).

Examples

prod(depolarizing_channel(0.01)(i) for i in 1:10)
depolarizing_channel(0.02, 2)(1, 2) * controlled(Z)(1, 2)
source
TensorMixedStates.depolarizing_dissipator — Function
depolarizing_dissipator(γ, n = 1)

the generator depolarizing n sites together at rate γ, reset_dissipator(γ, n, "FullyMixed"), $\gamma\,(\mathrm{tr}_S\rho \otimes I/d^n - \rho)$. Evolving under it for a time t is depolarizing_channel(1 - exp(-γt), n).

Examples

-im * H + sum(depolarizing_dissipator(0.1)(i) for i in 1:10)
source
TensorMixedStates.dephasing_channel — Function
dephasing_channel(p, A = Level)

the channel dephasing with probability p in the eigenbasis of A, by default the basis of the site, $(1 - p)\,\rho + p\,\Delta(\rho)$, Δ being Dephase(A).

Examples

prod(dephasing_channel(0.01)(i) for i in 1:10)
dephasing_channel(0.1, Z ⊗ Z)(1, 2)      # the parity of two qubits
source
TensorMixedStates.dephasing_dissipator — Function
dephasing_dissipator(γ, A = Level)

the generator dephasing at rate γ in the eigenbasis of A, by default the basis of the site, γ * Dissipator(Dephase(A)), $\gamma\,(\Delta(\rho) - \rho)$, Δ being Dephase(A). Every coherence between two eigenspaces decays at the same rate γ, where Dissipator(A) damps it at $(\lambda_a - \lambda_b)^2 / 2$, faster for distant eigenvalues: the two agree when A has two eigenvalues, Dissipator(Z) being dephasing_dissipator(2, Z). Evolving under it for a time t is dephasing_channel(1 - exp(-γt), A).

Examples

-im * H + sum(dephasing_dissipator(0.1)(i) for i in 1:10)
dephasing_dissipator(0.1, Z ⊗ Z)(1, 2)      # the parity of two qubits
source
TensorMixedStates.amplitude_damping_channel — Function
amplitude_damping_channel(p, L = Sp)

the channel of amplitude_damping_dissipator(γ, L) over a time t, p = 1 - exp(-γt) being the probability that an excitation of a Qubit, a Fermion or a Boson decays. On a qubit, its Kraus operators are Id - (1 - sqrt(1 - p)) * N and sqrt(p) * Sp. For p = 1, an infinite time, it is the reset to the state the decay ends in, refused when there is none.

Examples

prod(amplitude_damping_channel(0.01)(i) for i in 1:10)
amplitude_damping_channel(0.1, A)(3)           # each photon lost with probability 0.1
source
TensorMixedStates.amplitude_damping_dissipator — Function
amplitude_damping_dissipator(γ, L = Sp)

the generator of the decay at rate γ of the excitations the operator L of one site destroys, γ * Dissipator(L): Sp on a Qubit, from "Dn" to "Up", C on a Fermion, A on a Boson. Evolving under it for a time t is amplitude_damping_channel(1 - exp(-γt), L).

Examples

-im * H + sum(amplitude_damping_dissipator(0.1)(i) for i in 1:10)
amplitude_damping_dissipator(0.1, A)(3)     # the loss of photons

NDTensors.matrix — Function
matrix(op, site, sites...)

the matrix of the generic operator op on the given sites, a single one standing for several identical ones, the last site varying fastest. A tensor product of fermionic factors holds the Jordan-Wigner strings between them: the matrix of C ⊗ dag(C) is that of C(1) * dag(C)(2). A channel or a generator acts on the density matrices ρ of the sites: its matrix is that of the map of vec(ρ), the last site varying fastest and, on each site, the ket faster than the bra.

Examples

matrix(X ⊗ A, Qubit(), Boson(2))
matrix(Gate(X), Qubit())
source
matrix(op, system)

the matrix of the operator op, placed on sites, on all the sites of system, the Jordan-Wigner strings of its fermionic operators laid, on the basis of the product states ordered as kron orders them, the first site varying the slowest, as dense_state and Vector order them: the matrix an exact diagonalization of a small system checks a result against. It is refused beyond the dimension dense_max, 4096.

Examples

Gates of circuits

The module Circuits, using .Circuits, gathers the gates of quantum circuits: H, S and T, which H and S extend to qudits, Phase, Sum, the rotations and the controlled gates. A rotation by θ has θ/2 in its exponent, Rx(θ) being $e^{-i\theta X/2}$ on a qubit and Rxx(θ) $e^{-i\theta X \otimes X/2}$ on two, as in OpenQASM, Qiskit or PennyLane.
